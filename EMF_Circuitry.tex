\section{Forces} 

\subsection{Lorenz Force}

Forces from a moving particles (Lorenz Forces). Bend $\vec{v}$ into $\vec{b}$ to get the direction for $\vec{F}_B$ 

\[\vec{F}_B = q(\vec{v} \times \vec{b})\]

Objects moving in a circle. 

\begin{itemize}
    \item $\vec{F}_B = \vec{F}_C$
    \item $r = \frac{mv}{qb}$
    \item Pitch: Distance parallel to the magnetic field
    \item Period of Orbit $v = \frac{2\pi r}{T}$; $T = \frac{2 \pi r}{v}$
\end{itemize}


\subsection{Currents and Torques of Wires}

$\vec{L}$ is in direction of the current. Field wraps around a wire (RHR it).

$$F_b = i |\vec{L} \times \vec{B} |$$

Torque in a wire loop (probably derivable), with a and b being with width and height respectively: 

$$\tau = ib (\text{area of loop})$$

\section{Law of BS}

$$\delta \vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \frac{i \delta \vec{L} \times \hat{r}}{r^2}$$

Where $\delta \vec{L}$ is the vectorized length of wire, $\hat{r}$ is the unit vector from $\delta \vec{L}$ top, and r is the distance from the point to the wire.

Field of a wire on a point:

$$\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0 i}{2 \pi R}$$

Field of a enclosed wire loop:

$$\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{2R}$$

\section{Ampere's Law}

$$\oint \vec{B} \cdot \delta \vec{s} = \mu_0 i_{\text{enc}}$$

Amperian Loop: draw a path around an object that mimics the symmetry of the emitter. 

\section{Magnetic Flux}

\textbf{NOTE}: Be able to find flux from relate to velocity, change in area, work, and other forces

\begin{enumerate}
    \item (Law of Flux) The voltage induced in a coil is equal to negative of the rate of change of magnetic flux through a coil ($\epsilon = - N\frac{\delta \Phi}{\delta t} = -N\frac{\int B \delta A}{t}$)
    \item (Lenz's Law) An induced current has a direction such that the magnetic field due to the current opposes the charge in the magnetic  flux that induces the current. 
\end{enumerate}
